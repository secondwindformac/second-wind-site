When Second Wind is NOT for you

  • If you need Apple apps (Final Cut, Logic, iMessage, AirDrop) or apps that only exist for macOS: look at OpenCore Legacy Patcher.
  • If your Mac has the Apple T2 chip (2018 and later) or Apple Silicon: not supported yet (the installer stops before erasing anything).
  • If you cannot back up your files: Second Wind replaces macOS and erases the Mac.
Is Second Wind macOS?

No. It is Ubuntu 24.04 LTS set up to look and behave like a Mac. It contains no Apple software, logos or fonts.

How much does Second Wind cost?

Second Wind is free forever: the Mac look, the drivers, the app store and the updates. Mac Experience (the ⌘ keyboard shortcuts and the ⌘Space search) is free for 30 days and then costs US$10 once; it is not a subscription. Without Mac Experience the Mac still looks like a Mac.

Which is better for an old Mac, Second Wind or OpenCore Legacy Patcher?

It depends on what you need. OpenCore Legacy Patcher installs real macOS and keeps Apple's apps, but the process is technical and macOS 26 is Apple's last version for Intel Macs. Second Wind installs Ubuntu with a Mac feel, no terminal, drivers ready and security updates until 2029.

Which Macs work with Second Wind?

Intel Macs from 2013 to 2017: MacBook Air (tested end to end), MacBook Pro Retina and iMac from 2013 to 2015. MacBook Pro 2016-2017 is in development; Macs with the T2 chip and Apple Silicon are not supported.
